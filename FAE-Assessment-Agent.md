# FAE Assessment Agent

## Objective

Support Field Application Engineers in evaluating customer opportunities, technical requirements, and business potential using AI-assisted workflows.

## Inputs

- Customer requirements
- Technical specifications
- Material and technology requirements
- Commercial information
- Project timelines

## Outputs

- Opportunity qualification assessment
- Technical risk evaluation
- Recommended next actions
- Information gaps and follow-up questions
- Summary for sales and engineering teams

## Example Workflow

1. Receive customer requirements
2. Review technical needs
3. Assess technology fit
4. Identify risks and gaps
5. Generate recommendations
6. Prepare customer-facing summary

## Benefits

- Faster opportunity assessment
- Improved consistency
- Better customer responsiveness
- Structured decision-making
- Reduced manual effort

## Technologies

- Microsoft Copilot Studio
- Power Automate
- SharePoint
- Excel
- AI Prompt Engineering

## Status

Concept and learning project focused on applying AI to sales engineering and customer engagement workflows.
